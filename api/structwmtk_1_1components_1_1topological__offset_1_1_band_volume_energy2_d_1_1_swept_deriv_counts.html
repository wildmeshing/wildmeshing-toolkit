Detailed Description

set_swept()'s counts, 3D's SweptDerivCounts: the search model's derivative evaluations, of them those with an edge part labelled by rule (a), those taken from the swept triangles (grad_hess(), at x != x0) and those whose Hessian is the edges' hess_swept (at x0), and those taken from the ring's band faces (front_edge_derivs() threw).
