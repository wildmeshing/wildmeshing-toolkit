Detailed Description

The swap in flight on this thread, as candidate_energy() and the sanity check read it (see tet_energy(), CANDIDATE CELLS). Filled by swap_record_fill(), which swap_before_interior() and swap_before_surface() call once they have accepted the ring – in the engine's swap_edge_before(), swap_edge_44_before() and swap_edge_56_before(), all of which run before the connectivity changes and so before the 4-4 / 5-6 case search, and in swap_face_before() before its gate. Cleared at the top of both hooks, so no record outlives its swap into the next one on the thread. A 3-2 has no case search and no gate and gets no record.
