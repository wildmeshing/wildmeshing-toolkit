bool wmtk::components::topological_offset::TetAttributes::rest_valid = false

Rest shape (deform_others): the tet's corners when it last changed topologically, in the oriented order. Stamped for every deformable cell at release and re-stamped by the operation after-hooks for every cell an accepted split / collapse / swap changed, never by smoothing – a child left on its parent's rest reads det F ~ 1/2 and fights to regrow.
