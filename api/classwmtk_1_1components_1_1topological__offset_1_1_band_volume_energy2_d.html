Detailed Description

The 2D twin of BandVolumeEnergy3D: a vertex's part of the energy's band term (TopoOffsetTriMesh::band_cell_term()), the vertex moving: 

E(x) = sum over the vertex's band faces t of  A_t(x) * m_t(x),
m_t(x) = min over ALL input primitives P of (1/3) sum over t's 3 corners q of
         (d_P(q) - delta) / delta,

the three corners being x and the face's two fixed ones, A_t the face's signed area, positive on a valid face, the primitives ExactBandIntegral2D's (the input's segments and points) and the min its min_mean_distance(). Each face's term is band_cell_term() at x, so the solve descends exactly the face terms E sums.

A_t(x) = (q1 - x) . N / 2, N = (q2 - q1) turned clockwise, is affine in x: its gradient is -N / 2 and its Hessian zero. The gradient and Hessian of m_t are the minimising primitive P's, grad d_P(x) / (3 delta) and hess d_P(x) / (3 delta) (ExactBandIntegral2D::primitive_distance()), and the products are 3D's: grad E_t = m_t grad A + A grad m_t, hess E_t = grad A grad m_t^T + grad m_t grad A^T + A hess m_t.

set_exact_integral() (EXPERIMENTAL_band_volume_rule "exact"): A_t m_t becomes J_t / delta, J_t = ExactBandIntegral2D::value() of t with x a corner, its gradient and Hessian grad_hess() in that corner: exact, except on rule-(a) pieces (the bound's derivative) and at a kink of d on an edge of t through x (the one-sided limit for x moving into t). As 3D's.

Both rules keep the last point's minima, or its value and its derivatives, as 3D's: polysolve asks for each separately at one x, and again in its line search.

Where 2D differs: the corner bound searches every evaluation's minima afresh, through min_mean_distance(), which scans every primitive (2D has no input envelope to search a box in): no starting primitive and no certified ball (3D's ball_candidates()). The ball is one of 3D's two speed devices, proved bit-equal to a min_mean_distance() call per cell and evaluation (the two together MEASURED there: smoothing 68 s -> 15 s on the cube, the output bit-identical); 2D keeps the other, the last point's minima, and makes that call otherwise. No 2D measurement asks for the ball yet. "front_flux" has no 2D model and is refused (band_rule_exact()). "exact_swept" has 3D's search model (set_swept()).

EXPERIMENTAL_band_volume_rule "exact_swept": the smoother's search model is the function the move is judged on (TopoOffsetTriMesh::swept_accept()), 3D's BandVolumeEnergy3D::set_swept() one dimension down. Its value at x is swept_change(x0, x) over edges, x0 the position at construction: (E(B(x)) - E(B(x0))) / delta, E(B) = int_B chi (d - delta) dA, exactly where value() integrates every piece exactly. Its gradient and Hessian are E(B)'s at x: the sum over the edges of ExactBandIntegral2D:: front_edge_derivs() / delta. Each edge's normal stays out of B at every x the solver evaluates, where no ring face is inverted. Defined at x = x0 too.

Where an edge holds a part labelled by rule (a), or a swept triangle a rule-(a) piece, the derivatives are those of the judged change itself, as 3D's: at x0 the edges' gradient and their hess_swept, elsewhere the sum over the swept triangles of sign_f ExactBandIntegral2D::grad_hess(conv(x0, x, a_f), 1) / delta. Where front_edge_derivs() throws (its parts do not tile the edge), the "exact" rule's: the sum over the ring's band faces of grad_hess(t, 0) / delta. Every derivative evaluation is counted into counts. The last point's value, and its gradient and Hessian, are kept. See 3D's set_swept() for the measurements behind each branch (the edges' derivatives at x with rule (a) are no gradient: 3D's summed Hessian 1e-2 or more asymmetric in 449 of 3416 solves).

Where 2D differs: the swept triangles' derivatives partition them again (3D reads the partitions its value made, a speed device; the 2D integral keeps none).
