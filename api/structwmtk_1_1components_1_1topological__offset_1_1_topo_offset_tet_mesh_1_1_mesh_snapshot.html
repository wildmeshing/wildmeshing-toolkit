Detailed Description

An in-memory image of the mesh, to put it back as it was (for growth_mode "smart": the revert after a failed attempt). Taken on a consolidated mesh: the tets in tid order, each as oriented_tet_vids() gives it – the order the face and edge slots (4 tid + j, 6 tid + j) are keyed on – and copies of every per-element collection over the live slots: the vertex group (m_vertex_attribute, m_vertex_extra), the face group (m_face_attribute, m_face_extra), the edge group (m_edge_attribute) and m_tet_attribute.
