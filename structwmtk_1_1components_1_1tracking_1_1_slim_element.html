Detailed Description

A triangle of a 2D layout and the shape it should keep.

Its energy is the symmetric Dirichlet energy |J|^2 + |J^-1|^2 of the map J from the rest triangle to its layout, times weight. A rest is given per element, not per vertex, so two elements sharing a vertex may want different shapes of it – the same outline vertex is in a patch before an edit and in the patch after it.
