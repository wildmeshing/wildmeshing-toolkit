Detailed Description

The input's own curves, when the envelopes are built around the input (envelope_reference "input") and the surface inserted is not the input: a simplification ran, and without preserve_topology it glues rims of the input away and makes rims and non-manifold edges of its own.

Set, tag_order2_edges() builds the tubes around order2 – per input, the edges of the input's own faces holds_order2_edge() holds – and holds there an edge that lies in the tube of every input of its set; anchor_feature_points() centres the ball of a curve end or junction on the nearest of points when one lies in it. Measured on the tetwild integration configs (2026-10): 8 carried order-2 curves on no curve of the input (crown, 1017012, 101168, 104187, 104188, 104509 and 111388 entirely, 314748 18%), and of 678 anchored curve ends and junctions, 605 were on such curves.

What the simplification made – an order-2 edge of one input outside those tubes, a curve end or junction with none of points in reach – is held around itself as the insertion leaves it: in a tube around its own curve, in a ball at its own position. Not for the input's sake, but because the one-sided surface envelope cannot keep a curve from eroding from its end, whoever made it, and the input on that curve erodes with it. Left to the surface envelope alone, measured (2026-11, input samples farther than eps from the output, held -> left free): tetwild 104509 239 -> 317, crown 554 -> 695, 104187 1058 -> 1222, 104188 962 -> 1105; triwild 202260 927 -> 1315, 191265 148259 -> 181805, 196407 2.29M -> 2.41M of 2.54M. An order-2 edge where two or more inputs meet is held the same way: the input's curve there is the intersection of the inputs, which only an arrangement of the original would give (until 2026-11 such an edge was left to the surface envelopes). What the simplification removed is lost, as the topology change it is allowed to make.

Unset, the tagged edges and their vertices as the insertion leaves them are the input's – they are, with no simplification before the insertion – and the tubes and anchors are built around them. Set by the application before init, by set_input_curves().
