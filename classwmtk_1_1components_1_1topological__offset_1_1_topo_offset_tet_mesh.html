bool edge_split_sphere_trace (const Vector3d &p_in, const Vector3d &p_out, Vector3d &p_new, size_t &steps) const
 Construction placement in the marching: sphere tracing along the edge from p_in (the endpoint in the input complex, label != 0) towards p_out (the background endpoint) for the point where d(x) = D, D = m_construction_distance and d(x) the distance to the input complex through m_input_complex_bvh. From t = 0 the trace evaluates d at the current point and steps forward by D - d, the largest step that cannot cross the level set (d is 1-Lipschitz); it stops when |d - D| <= sphere_trace_target_rel_tol x D and returns true with p_new there. It returns false, p_new untouched, as soon as the current point reaches or passes p_out (t >= L: the level set is not on the edge) or would move behind p_in (d(p_in) already beyond the target); the caller then places the plain midpoint. Every step taken is longer than the tolerance, so the trace ends within L / (tol x D) steps; steps returns how many it took. No snapping away from the endpoints: a point found arbitrarily close to p_out is used as is.

Detailed Description

The offset's tet mesh, on the shared 3D optimizer.

Mirrors TopoOffsetTriMesh one dimension up: the construction phase (simplicial embedding, marching tets, growing the band) is entirely its own, and the optimization phase that follows is wmtk::TetOptimizerMesh's, with the offset supplying only policy through the hooks.

Two surfaces are tracked. Every tag-region boundary (input complex and domain wall included) keeps the primary class 0 and is held in its tags' envelopes, as tetwild holds its input; the offset surface is OFFSET_SURFACE_CLASS, the faces across which the incident tet labels differ. Class-0 faces may move within their tubes; only the offset one is driven toward target_distance.

◆ band_cell_term()

double wmtk::components::topological_offset::TopoOffsetTetMesh::band_cell_term ( const std::array< size_t, 4 > &  vids) const
inline

b(t), one band cell's part of int_B (d - delta) / delta dV, as an upper bound: 

vol * min over ALL input triangles P of (1/4) sum over the 4 corners q of
      (d_P(q) - delta) / delta,

d_P the distance to input triangle P alone, delta = target_distance, vol the cell's unsigned volume (the cell is valid wherever this is read). An upper bound on the exact vol * mean of (d - delta)/delta for every P: d <= d_P, and the convex d_P lies below its linear interpolant. A function of the four corners alone, and no split raises it: each child's min includes the parent's minimiser, whose finer interpolant lies lower. Exact where one triangle's distance is affine on the cell. Signed: about -vol for a cell at the input, positive beyond the level set. A volume, as vol AMIPS^3 is. One input region only (the triangles are the whole input's): refused otherwise.

Why the corner bound rather than a quadrature of d over the cell: corners on the input read (d - delta)/delta = -1 whatever the cell's interior is, so the mean over the four corners and the centroid read a band tet with all four corners on the input as -1.57 against a dense +4.83, and the whole band as -41.0 against a dense +13.1 (one-slot model, stage 1, cells about 10x target_distance). Why the min over ALL triangles rather than over each corner's nearest plus a triangle stored on the cell at the last split pass: over 2000 band cells with a front vertex on the last frames of the cube (24 triangles) and 100026 (1736), the corners' nearest missed the min in 0.1% and 4.5% of the cells, by up to 23 and 70 bars – corners lie on input edges and vertices, where triangles tie – and the term depended on how the cell was made. No triangle outside the corners' nearest (ties included) had a smaller mean, so the exact search (InputTriangles::min_mean_distance()) visits boxes only.

That is EXPERIMENTAL_band_volume_rule "corner_bound", the default. Under "exact" b(t) is J_t / delta, J_t = int_t (d - delta) dV in closed form (ExactBandIntegral::value()): exact up to rounding, and an upper bound only on the pieces of t where two features' regions meet on a curved border (rule (a)). See the spec doc.

◆ offset_surface_foldover_labels()

std::vector< char > wmtk::components::topological_offset::TopoOffsetTetMesh::offset_surface_foldover_labels ( ) const

Per-vertex 0/1: is this vertex an endpoint of a COLLAPSED (folded-over) offset surface edge? Debug-frame diagnostic; see write_vtu(). Costs one pass over the live offset faces, no field evaluation.

An offset-surface edge carries two live offset faces. Measured through either side, the angle between them is 180 degrees where the surface is flat and 360 where the two faces lie on top of each other with that side pinched to nothing. Over FOLDOVER_OUTER_ANGLE_DEG through EITHER side is the fold, and every such edge's two endpoints get 1.

Which side is pinched is deliberately not determined: on the cube the measured folds pinch the BACKGROUND, not the band, so a test written around a pinched band found none of them. Since the two sides sum to 360, the test is simply that the unsigned angle is under 360 minus the threshold. Vertices of an edge that does not carry exactly two live offset faces are left 0, as are degenerate faces: this is a diagnostic, and a number it cannot measure is not a fold.

The 2D twin is TopoOffsetTriMesh::offset_surface_foldover_labels(), which asks the same question of a curve vertex's two incident offset edges.

◆ tet_energy()

double wmtk::components::topological_offset::TopoOffsetTetMesh::tet_energy ( size_t  tid) const

THE per-tet energy E(t), read from the mesh as it is – the cell's corners and its label, nothing hypothetical. E = the sum of E(t) over the live cells (global_energy()) is the one energy of the 3D loop. 

tet_energy(t) = cell_energy(oriented_tet_vids(t), get_quality(t), cell_is_offset_band(t))

cell_energy(vids, A^3, band) = MAX_ENERGY                          if A^3 >= MAX_ENERGY
                             = (w / s^3) vol A^3 + [band] b(vids)  otherwise

w = offset_amips_weight, s = stop_energy (optimization_stop_metric()), so a cell at the stop energy costs about w vol. A^3 is the base's TetOptimizerMesh::get_quality(), the AMIPS^3 the engine stores as the cell quality; its MAX_ENERGY (unscoreable) is the cell's energy. vol A^3 is vol_amips3(), from the exact AMIPS^3 and the edge lengths, never from a determinant. b is band_cell_term(): the band cell's part of int_B (d - delta) / delta dV, as an upper bound that no split raises – vol times the min over all input triangles P of the corner mean of (d_P - delta) / delta. Both terms are volumes, so E converges under refinement. Before the march, while the repulsion runs (m_repulsion_potential set), a cell also carries repulsion_cell_term(); there is no band then. A cell's energy depends on its own four corners and its label alone: an operation changes the energy of the cells it changes and of no other.

WHERE IT IS COMPARED: always as the SUM of tet_energy() over the cells an operation changes, before against after, the cells summed in the same order each time.

  • Collapse: before = the sum over v1's ring (collapse_before_vertex()); after = the sum over the survivor's ring less v2's old cells outside v1's ring, which keep their corners and labels (collapse_after_connectivity()). Refused unless after <= before (a tie passes, a NaN refuses). Not in coarsening.
  • Swaps (3-2, 4-4, 5-6, face swap, interior or a flip of a tracked surface): before = the sum over the replaced cells (swap_before_interior() / swap_before_surface()); refused unless the sum over the new cells, labels written, is STRICTLY below it (swap_after_cells()). Strict, so a swap pass terminates.
  • Smoothing (smooth_front_vertex(), smooth_nonfront_vertex(), smooth_repulsion_vertex()): the sum over the vertex's ring after the move may not exceed the sum before.
  • Split: no rule, unless EXPERIMENTAL_split_energy_gate (the children's sum strictly below the parents'). The engine's own AMIPS^3 rules for collapses and swaps are off: collapse_quality_allowed() and swap_quality_allowed() admit everything, and this energy decides in their place.

CANDIDATE CELLS: the 4-4 and 5-6 case searches (TetMesh::swap_edge_44() / swap_edge_56(), scored through swap_edge_44_energy() / swap_edge_56_energy()) and the face swap's gate (swap_face_before()) score cells that do not exist yet, by the same sum: the current cells by the before-sum (op_case 0), a candidate configuration by the sum of candidate_energy() over its cells – cell_energy() at the candidate's four positions (a swap moves no vertex) with the label swap_after_cells() will write (the ring's one label for an interior swap, the side of a ring vertex the cell contains for a flip). A candidate is taken only when its sum is strictly below the current cells'. swap_after_cells() then applies the rule again on the real cells and stays THE rule; under perform_sanity_checks it compares the committed configuration's score with the sum it reads (swap_scoring_check()). Until 2026-09-28 the search and the gate scored AMIPS^3 alone, so a swap that lowers the energy while raising AMIPS was discarded before the rule saw it: at the cube 1e-3, 93.5% of the valence-4 surface edges whose flip would cut the front's error raised max AMIPS.

THE SMOOTHER minimises the vertex's part of E (smoothing_extra_energy()): the AMIPS term over its ring (amips3_energy()) and, unless an input envelope holds the vertex, the band term of its band cells (band_volume_energy_at()) – the held-vertex exception is measured there. Every move is then judged by the ring's sum of this energy: a vertex held on a surface through the engine's projected step, which takes the first projected position where that sum drops (smoothing_ring_energy()); every other vertex through the veto after its solve (smooth_front_vertex(), smooth_nonfront_vertex()).

The front's face measure (face_offset_term()) is not part of E: the exit, the refinement and the frames read it.
