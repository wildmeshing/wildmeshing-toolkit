Detailed Description

While one is alive on a thread, a piece of a face (front_face(), front_face_derivs()) that a curved bisector crosses is labelled by value()'s rule (a) in the limit of a thin swept tet, instead of throwing. The swept tet conv(x, x + t v, a, b) of the face f = (x, a, b) moving its corner kv = x has height t (v.n) phi over f, phi the hat function of x, so value()'s pieces of it are the pieces of f times that height, and its rule (a) keeps on such a piece the member g of smallest int d_g dV = t (v.n) int_R d_g phi dA + O(t^2). Here: the piece R (members S, after prune_points()) is split by the members' side planes as value()'s side_split() splits its piece; a part on the offset side of every member takes the member of smallest int_part d_g phi dA (the first in S on a tie, as value()); a part where they disagree takes the side of the member nearest at its centroid (nearest_member()), and if that is the offset side the member of smallest integral among those putting it there; else it is dropped. The part is then labelled with that member as any other (labeled()). So summed over the band's boundary faces through x, front_face_derivs() at x is the exact derivative at x of the band change "exact_swept" judges a move from x on (the sum of value() over the swept tets), where value() and the face's labelling cut alike. Until 2026-11 they did not where two edge members cross with a planar bisector (value() took rule (a) there, the labelling split the piece exactly); both now split it by the same planes (resolve()), which on a rod is the inside of every bend.

"exact_swept"'s search model opens one (BandVolumeEnergy3D::set_swept(); before, it took the ring cells' derivatives where a face threw). MEASURED (test_exact_swept's curved-bisector fixture, 2026-10-23): at x0, against central differences of the judged swept change, gradient 1.5e-11 relative; the ring cells' 2.2e-2.
