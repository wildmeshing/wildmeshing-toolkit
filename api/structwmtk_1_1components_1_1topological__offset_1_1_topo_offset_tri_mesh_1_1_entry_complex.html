Detailed Description

Per In entry, its complex (the selection's boundary) by vertex ids, as label_input_complex() labels it: what init_input_complex_bvh() extracts for that entry alone when entries touch, an edge they share once for each. Empty for other entries. 3D's EntryComplex, one dimension down: the boundary of a 2D selection has no face.
