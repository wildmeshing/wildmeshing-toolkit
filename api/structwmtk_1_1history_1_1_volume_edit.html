Detailed Description

One operation that changed the tracked surface of a tet mesh, as the tets around it saw it: the tets before and after, their regions, and where their vertices were.

Vertices are local to the edit, numbered from 0: a volume map follows points in space, so no vertex identity is needed from one edit to the next. The edit links to the surface history by the range of its edits the operation made, and by the surface vertex of each local vertex, so the tracked faces among the tets, and the surface map's local map of the operation, can be found there.
