Detailed Description

A boundary edge of the band B through the moving vertex x, the 2D twin of a 3D boundary face's (a_f, b_f): its other end a, and the pair it makes with x ordered so that the pair's normal rot_cw(second - first) points out of B: (x, a) when x_first, (a, x) otherwise (TopoOffsetTriMesh::band_swept_edges()). 3D orders a face by its two other corners; an edge has one, so the order is this flag.
