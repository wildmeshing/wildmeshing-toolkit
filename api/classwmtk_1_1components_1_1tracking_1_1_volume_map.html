Detailed Description

The map between the first and the last state of a tet mesh's volume, region by region, rebuilt from its surface history and its volume history (history::VolumeHistory).

An operation that does not change the tracked surface leaves every region where it is: the identity in space. One that does gets a local map on the tets around it, built per region from pairs of patches (before -> after): each patch embedded star-shaped – its real geometry when it is, else a fallback embedding (see volume::spherical) – and points carried along rays from the centres by the boundary map, the identity on the faces the operation keeps and the surface map's local map (SurfaceMap, CollapsePatch::removed_star) on the tracked faces it changes. Both sides of the surface use the same surface map, so they agree on it. A collapse whose side has a chord goes through a decomposition (split, collapse, unsplit), a swap that cannot be one pair through an exact split and a collapse.

The const queries – to_output(), to_input(), build_all(), op_kinds(), stats() – may be called on one map from several threads at once: what they share, the operations built and kept and the stats, is kept under a mutex, and each call counts its points' stats apart and adds them at its end.
