Detailed Description

A face over the bar with all three corners placed: a, b are the ends of its LONGEST edge (the chord the target is derived from), c the third corner; len the longest edge's length.

measure is the face's MEAN sag as a LENGTH (the ratio times the tube). Nothing reads it today – refinement is the halving, which needs the face's corners alone – and it is kept because the sag condition is still being reworked.
