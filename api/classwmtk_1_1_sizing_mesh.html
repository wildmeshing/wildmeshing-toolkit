Detailed Description

A target edge length that varies over space: the sizing_mesh option of tetwild, triwild, simwild and isotropic_remeshing.

A mesh whose vertices carry the absolute target length as the vertex field target_length (one positive value per vertex), interpolated linearly inside its cells:

  • Planar: its triangles, read in the xy plane. A point outside every triangle is not covered.
  • Volume: its tetrahedra. A point outside every tet is not covered.
  • Surface: its triangles in 3D, at the point of the surface closest to the query. Every point is covered.

The queries only read, so threads may share one.
