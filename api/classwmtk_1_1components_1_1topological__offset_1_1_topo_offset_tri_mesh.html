 The fold predicate, the one test of what a fold is: the 2D twin of TopoOffsetTetMesh::fold_test(), one dimension down. A vertex of the offset curve with exactly two offset edges, their normals out of the band n0 and n1, is a fold when the normals are within angle (foldover_angle) of opposite – the two edges lying on top of each other, the tip of a zero-area sheet – and at least one of the two edges is turned from the field: its angle to the field's normal, field_angle(0) or field_angle(1) (front_edge_field_angle()), over angle, or not measurable (NaN), so that nothing unmeasured passes for no fold. Two opposite edges that both follow the field are no fold but a ridge vertex (3D's ridge edge): the front following a corner of the level set sharper than angle, which is where it belongs. field_angle is asked only of an opposite pair. The normals' orientation does not decide the opposite test: n0 . n1 is minus the cosine of the angle between the two edges' directions from the vertex, whichever side the band is on, so a sheet of the band and one of the background are caught alike.

Detailed Description

The offset's 2D mesh, on the shared 2D optimizer.

Mirrors TopoOffsetTetMesh: the construction phase is entirely its own, and the optimization phase that follows is wmtk::TriOptimizerMesh's.

Two surfaces are tracked. Every tag-region boundary (input complex and domain wall included) keeps the primary class 0 and is held in its tags' envelopes, as triwild holds its input; the offset boundary is OFFSET_SURFACE_CLASS, in 2D exactly the edges across which the incident face labels differ, so label_offset_boundary() derives it rather than storing it. Class-0 edges may move within their tubes; only the offset one is driven toward target_distance.

Which boundaries the region-class envelopes hold, and how they are built.

PerTag (deform_others false): one exact tube per input tag around that tag's boundary segments, the domain wall in the tags of its wall faces. WallComplex (deform_others true): exactly two tubes, the domain wall and the boundary of the input complex (any dimension, any manifoldness: it is a set of segments), under the pseudo-tags m_wall_tag / m_complex_tag. Either way build_boundary_envelopes() derives the masks and tubes from the mesh as it stands when called: at load (PerTag, before the complex is labelled) and when deform_others switches the setup at construction; not at the final pass, which holds the region boundaries where the load did, as 3D's. The offset tube is separate and unchanged.

◆ front_pocket_split()

void wmtk::components::topological_offset::TopoOffsetTriMesh::front_pocket_split ( const std::string &  where)

EXPERIMENTAL_front_pocket_split: the 2D twin of TopoOffsetTetMesh::front_pocket_split(), one dimension down. Invariant: no cell is PINNED when a smoothing pass of the loop starts. A pinned cell is a triangle outside the input complex, band or not, whose three corners are all front vertices (VertexExtra2d::m_is_on_offset).

Why, as in 3D: only smoothing moves vertices, and no vertex joins or leaves the front during a smoothing pass, so a triangle can only be flattened during smoothing, and only from one that is pinned when the pass starts. The offset term pulls a pinned triangle's three corners onto the level set, which then fixes its shape – the chord of a curve and a third point on it, a sliver – and where the level set curves the wrong way flattens it to zero area, a fold of the front; a triangle with a corner off the front has a vertex the AMIPS term alone moves, which keeps it shaped.

How. Each pinned triangle has its longest edge that is not a front edge (edge_is_offset_surface_live() false) split by the loop's own split, the next longest when that is refused. The midpoint is off the front and a corner of both children, so such a split makes no pinned cell and removes one. A pinned triangle with all three edges on the front, a one-triangle component of its label, has no such edge: it is split on its longest front edge, and the next round splits the edge from that midpoint (on the front) to the third corner, an edge off the front of both children – a vertex off the front inside it, as 3D's six-edge split puts one on a face (see the scan in Optimize2d.cpp for the measurement); 3D's twin is its cell with all four faces on the offset surface. EXPERIMENTAL_front_spanned_split (see the spec): every SPANNED edge – both ends front vertices, not a front edge, no triangle at it in the input complex – is split first, at its midpoint, off the front; the spanned triangle is the pinned one, and 3D's spanned face is 2D's spanned edge (codimension 1, every corner on the front, not on the front itself). Rescanned until nothing is left to split, at most kPocketMaxRounds rounds. One [pocket split] line, where naming the pass. Before all of it, the chords the last settled turn listed under EXPERIMENTAL_settle_mark "vertex_split" (m_settle_split_edges) are split, with one [settle split] line, as in 3D. A no-op with the key off and in the final pass (m_freeze_front; 3D: a TetWild pass).

) const

For every element above filter_energy, why its edges cannot be split.

log_stuck_refine_census() answers "what are the bad elements"; this answers "what is stopping the mesh from fixing them", the question that matters when the final pass refines somewhere else instead. Attributes each of a bad face's three edges to the first gate that refuses it, in the order the code applies them (TriOptimizerMeshSplit.cpp):

short length^2 < splitting_l2 * mean(sizing)^2 – never even offered to the queue. The remedy is the sizing field, not the split. valence a link vertex is over split_high_valence_threshold. Two shortcuts, both stated so the output is not over-read:

Each bad face is also located: centroid, distance to the input complex, and Phi/c there, which separates a collided corridor between two fronts from somewhere in the background.

◆ log_stuck_refine_census()

double  filter_energy 
)

Why the final pass is stuck: a census of the faces stuck-refine is about to chase.

Runs from refine_sizing_around_worst(), which only fires once max energy has stalled. The question is whether refinement is even the right response, so it separates four things the single MAX_ENERGY sentinel fuses:

  • exactly inverted (is_inverted) vs merely float-degenerate (is_inverted_f only). The second is a valid triangle AMIPS2D cannot score because m_posf lost the area – a rounding problem, not a geometry one, and splitting it makes two of them.
  • carrying an unrounded vertex, where m_posf is the wrong number outright.
  • already below the split gate, so the next pass cannot split them at all and lowering the sizing field is pure waste.
  • at the sizing floor, where apply_sizing_refinement has nothing left to give.

Plus where they are: class distribution, connected clusters, and how much the set overlaps the previous call's (quantised on a grid, because fids are recycled and cannot be compared across passes). A high overlap with a low cluster count says the pass is chasing the same few spots forever; a scattered, changing set says something is manufacturing new degeneracies as fast as they are refined.

"exact_swept" (EXACT SWEPT CHANGE).

EXACT SWEPT CHANGE (EXPERIMENTAL_band_volume_rule "exact_swept", 3D's default since 455ab9ba66): the 2D twin of TopoOffsetTetMesh's. The energy is "exact"'s – b(t) = J_t / delta, J_t = ExactBandIntegral2D::value() – but an operation's rule does not integrate its faces before and after: it compares the AMIPS terms of the faces it changes (band_term_cancels() is true for every one) plus the CHANGE of the band term E* / delta, E* = int_B (d - delta) dA, B the union of the band faces, which lives only where B changes and is integrated exactly there by value() on small triangles:

  • a vertex v moving from x0 to x1 (smoothing, a collapse's v1 moving onto v2): dE* = sum over the edges f of the boundary of B through v of sign_f value(conv(x0, x1, a_f)), a_f f's other end, sign_f = +1 where B grows (x1 beyond f, away from the band face f bounds). Why: a band face t = [v, a, p] moved to [x1, a, p] differs from [x0, a, p] by the 2-chain [x0, x1, a] - [x0, x1, p] (the boundary of the 3-simplex [x0, x1, a, p] is a 2-cycle, and a 2-cycle in the plane integrates every function to 0), one oriented triangle per edge of t through v, each edge as it bounds t. Over the band faces the edges between two of them cancel; the boundary edges of B are left, each with the oriented triangle [x0, x1, a_f], whose integral is sign_f times the unsigned one. Exact for mixed signs and for any move that inverts no face; a collapse's faces through v2 become flat and drop out the same way. A vertex on no boundary edge of B: 0.
  • a swap: 0. 2D's only swap is the interior flip (the engine refuses every tracked edge, so both faces share one label, which the new faces keep), which keeps B's region and boundary; 3D's band_chain_change() serves its surface flips, which 2D does not have.

◆ tri_energy()

double wmtk::components::topological_offset::TopoOffsetTriMesh::tri_energy ( size_t  fid,
bool  band_term = true 
) const

THE per-face energy E(t), read from the mesh as it is – the face's corners and its label, nothing hypothetical. E = the sum of E(t) over the live faces is the one energy of the 2D loop. The 2D twin of TopoOffsetTetMesh::tet_energy(), term for term: 

tri_energy(t) = cell_energy(oriented_tri_vids(t), get_quality(t),
                            face_is_offset_band(t))

cell_energy(vids, A, band) = MAX_ENERGY                          if A >= MAX_ENERGY
                           = (w / s^3) area A^3 + [band] b(vids)  otherwise

w = offset_amips_weight, s = stop_energy (optimization_stop_metric()), so a face at the stop energy costs about w area. A is the base's TriOptimizerMesh::get_quality(): the AMIPS the 2D engine stores as the face quality and compares with stop_energy, the first power (3D's engine stores AMIPS^3 and compares its cube root); its MAX_ENERGY (unscoreable) is the face's energy. area A^3 is area_amips3(), from the exact AMIPS and the edge lengths, never from a determinant. b is band_cell_term(): the band face's part of int_B (d - delta) / delta dA, B the union of the band faces – exactly ("exact"), or as an upper bound that no split raises ("corner_bound"). Both terms are areas, so E converges under refinement. Before the march, while the repulsion runs (m_repulsion_potential set), a face also carries repulsion_cell_term(); there is no band then. A face's energy depends on its own three corners and its label alone: an operation changes the energy of the faces it changes and of no other.

WHY THE CUBE, AS IN 3D, AND NOT THE STORED QUALITY'S FIRST POWER. At fixed edge lengths AMIPS2D = tr(J^T J) / det J and area = (sqrt3/4) det J, so area A^p grows like A^(p-1) as a face flattens: p = 1 (area times the stored quality, the literal reading of 3D's "vol times the stored AMIPS^3") is constant there and no barrier; p = 3 grows like A^2 (3D's vol A^3 like A^(3/2)). 3D MEASURED the non-barrier (EXPERIMENTAL_amips_pow, 679a0f7ead, removed): vol AMIPS, which falls as a cell flattens, let the pre-march TetWild pass's first smoothing pass take max AMIPS from 19.9 to 8e15 on 126196.

WHERE IT IS COMPARED: always as the SUM of tri_energy() over the faces an operation changes, before against after, the faces summed in the same order each time:

  • Collapse: before = the sum over v1's ring (collapse_before_vertex()); after = the sum over the survivor's ring less v2's old faces outside v1's ring, which keep their corners and labels (collapse_edge_after()). Refused unless after <= before (a tie passes, a NaN refuses). Not in coarsening.
  • Swap (the 2D flip, (a,b,c) + (a,b,d) -> (a,c,d) + (b,c,d), always interior: the engine refuses every tracked edge, so both faces share one label, which the new faces keep): before = the sum over the two faces (swap_edge_before()); refused unless the sum over the two new faces is STRICTLY below it (swap_edge_after()). Strict, so a swap pass terminates.
  • Smoothing (smooth_vertex_under_energy(), smooth_repulsion_vertex()): the sum over the vertex's ring after the move may not exceed the sum before.
  • Split: no rule, unless EXPERIMENTAL_split_energy_gate (the children's sum strictly below the parents'). The engine's own AMIPS rules for collapses and swaps are off: collapse_quality_allowed() and swap_quality_allowed() admit everything, and this energy decides in their place.

THE SMOOTHER minimises the vertex's part of E (smoothing_extra_energy()): the AMIPS term over its ring (amips3_energy()) and, unless an input envelope holds the vertex on the input (vertex_is_held_on_input()), the band term of its band faces (band_volume_energy_at()). Every move is then judged by the ring's sum of this energy: a vertex held on a region boundary through the engine's projected step, which takes the first projected position where that sum drops (smoothing_ring_energy()); every other vertex through the veto after its solve (smooth_vertex_under_energy()). As in 3D.

WHERE 2D DIFFERS FROM 3D'S RULES, and why:

  • No TetWild rules (3D's m_op_rules) and no TetWild pass (3D's m_tetwild_pass): 2D has no optimize_construction pass, and its final pass (m_freeze_front) is the frozen-front pass 3D replaced; its operations read this energy, band term included.
  • BAND TERM THAT CANCELS (band_term_cancels()) is false for every operation but under "exact_swept", where every rule adds the band term's exact change instead (EXACT SWEPT CHANGE below), as in 3D.
  • use_rest_pose: no 2D plastic face reads a rest here; 2D's plastic medium is deform_others', hard-wired off (envelope_setup()).

A region is a connected piece, not a tag: one tag covering two pieces that never touch would make them share a field and bring that bridging back. ◆ m_needle_pre

mutable

Why smoothing does not lift a sliver's apex off its opposite edge.

Interleaved smoothing is on by default, so every needle-adjacent vertex is visited after every topological pass. These counters say what happens when it is:

  • offered : smooth_before() entered with a needle already in the one-ring
  • reached : the solve produced a candidate and smooth_after() saw it
  • fixed : that candidate actually dropped the ring's worst below kNeedleQuality
  • stationary: the candidate moved the vertex less than 1e-12 – the solve found nothing

offered minus reached is the search failing outright; reached minus fixed is a move being made that does not repair the sliver. The two have different causes and the fix for one is not the fix for the other, which is why they are counted separately.
