◆ inverted_ops

size_t wmtk::components::tracking::VolumeMapStats::inverted_ops = 0

Operations a tet of which, before or after, is flat or turned the other way round at the positions the history holds – an application's exact positions rounded to doubles, as tetwild's and simwild's vertices not rounded yet after the insertion: the tets there overlap, a position in them is in two, and a point mapped there and back may come back off, by up to the size of the tets around (docs/tracking.md, "Volumes").

◆ surface_not_kept

size_t wmtk::components::tracking::VolumeMapStats::surface_not_kept = 0

Operations whose map is a bijection of the volume that does not keep the tracked surface: a face the surface map's cells map lies inside a patch, not on its boundary – at the boundary of an open sheet, where both sides of the surface are one side of the patch, or where no pair keeping the surface could be built (a weld), and one pair over the whole operation maps it.
