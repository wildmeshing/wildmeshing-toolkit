Detailed Description

Why smoothing refused a vertex, one counter per site where it can say no.

The base's SmoothRejectCounters covers the rejections inside the shared smoother; these cover the sites before it, where the offset says no for its own reasons, plus the split of what reached the smoother into offset-surface and interior.
