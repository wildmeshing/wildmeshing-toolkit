Detailed Description

The collapse energy rule's before half (collapse_before_vertex()), compared by collapse_after_connectivity(). The cells a collapse changes are v1's ring (removed, or given v2 in place of v1): before is tet_energy_sum() over it. v2's other cells keep their slots, corners and labels and so their energy, and are left out of both sums (v2_only, their sorted tids), so the two sums differ by the changed cells alone, with no summation noise.
