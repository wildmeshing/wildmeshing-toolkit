Detailed Description

Manifold checks of a pure subcomplex K of a triangle or tet mesh: the cells (faces in 2D, tets in 3D) for which a test holds, by id, with all their simplices. A vertex or an edge is manifold in K if K is a manifold with boundary near it: near a vertex, K is a disk (2D) or a ball (3D), whole or cut by K's boundary; near an edge (3D), K's tets around it form a single wedge. A vertex or an edge on no cell of K is manifold. The space outside the mesh counts as outside K.

The tests are combinatorial: K's cells around the simplex are joined through the facets they share there, and K is manifold at it if they form one group (3D vertices: and so do the cells outside K, the space outside the mesh counted as one of them).

manifold_extraction labels the non-manifold vertices and edges of the selected tets' boundary; tight_seal_geom compares each tag's non-manifold vertices before and after its fill.

3D: whether vertex v is manifold in K: K's tets around v are joined through the faces at v, and so are the tets around v outside K, together with the space outside the mesh, which lies beyond each of v's faces on the mesh's boundary. Equivalently, K's link of v (the faces opposite v in K's tets) is a disk or the whole sphere around v. A vertex of an edge that is not manifold is not manifold either.
