Detailed Description

A point of a tet mesh: where it is, and its region, an index into the volume history's regions (VolumeHistory::regions, each a set of tags) – or kAnyRegion: the region of the tet holding it, where an operation's tets first do (a point on a surface between two regions then goes either way). A position that is not a number (the default) is no point: a map leaves it as it is.
