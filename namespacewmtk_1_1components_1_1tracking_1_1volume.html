 A fallback embedding of a cone from a boundary apex c: every vertex keeps its distance from c, and its direction from c is laid out again, on the sphere, so the cone is star-shaped about a point next to c. The directions live in the gnomonic plane of a direction d; the link's triangles rest in the shapes of their real spherical triangles (near-degenerate ones opened to a height of fat of their longest edge), the outline is kept star-shaped about the origin by a scaffold of fan triangles (SLIM). Then the directions and radii are polished by L-BFGS on the symmetric Dirichlet energy of the tets themselves. Nothing if T is not a cone with a disk for a link.

Detailed Description

The combinatorics of a volume map's local maps: patches of tets on the vertices of one operation (VolumeEdit's local ids, and virtual vertices past them).

The geometry of a volume map's local maps: patches embedded star-shaped, and the radial map between two of them.
