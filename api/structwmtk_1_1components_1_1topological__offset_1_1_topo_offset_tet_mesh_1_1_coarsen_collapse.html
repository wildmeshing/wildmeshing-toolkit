Detailed Description

The coarsening guard's before-half (collapse_before_vertex(), coarsen_mesh()'s collapses only), read back by collapse_after_connectivity(): the largest get_quality() (AMIPS^3) over v1's ring, the cells the collapse reshapes or removes; v2's cells outside that ring, which it leaves as they are; and the live offset faces through v1 or v2 (sorted vertex triples, sorted), every face the collapse can change.
