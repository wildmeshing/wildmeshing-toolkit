Detailed Description

The offset surface's residual. Every band vertex and every face sample counts toward the max and the average, pinned vertices included. The reachable/pinned split (see band_vertex_is_reachable()) is attribution: a max driven by a pinned vertex calls for a different construction, not more optimization.
