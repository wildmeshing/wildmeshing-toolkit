Detailed Description

One face's labelled parts, kept by the caller between front_face() calls on that face, as Partition keeps a cell's partition: the parts are a function of f's 9 coordinates, in their order, and of the input alone (not of delta or kv). A call whose f equals, bit for bit, the f they were made for (by this integral) reads them; any other call makes them anew into it. Why: the smoother asks for the value at x and then for the derivatives at the same x, each labelling every boundary face through x.
