Detailed Description

Conservative tests of a function over a whole cell (a triangle in 2D, a tet in 3D) by ball refinement. If f is 1-Lipschitz (a distance function, for one), a ball (circle, sphere) of radius rho around c holds only values within rho of f(c), so one evaluation decides the whole ball when f(c) is more than rho from the level. Otherwise the ball is refined: the ball through the corners of the cell's bounding square (cube) is replaced by the balls of its square's 2^dim sub-squares that overlap the cell, and so on down.

Moved here from topological_offset's Circle.hpp and Sphere.hpp, and tight_seal_geom's certify_on_cell, which used the same refinement.

Certifies that a 1-Lipschitz function f stays on one side of level over a whole cell, conservatively: true only if proven.

at_most: max over the cell of f <= level; otherwise: min over the cell of f > level. f is any 1-Lipschitz function, typically the distance to some set. A ball whose values are all on the right side, by the relative margin margin (so that rounding can only refute), is certified; the cell is refuted if a corner is not (corner_values: f at the corners, with the margin), or if a ball's centre lies in the cell with f on the wrong side; any other ball is refined into its children that overlap the cell, and refutes the cell once max_depth levels down. NaN refutes.
