Detailed Description

Helpers of the geometric tight seal, SimWildMeshTri::tight_seal_geom (AnnotationsTri.cpp) and SimWildMesh::tight_seal_geom (AnnotationsTet.cpp), which keep the operation itself. The helpers below need no mesh; those that do are private members of the two mesh classes, defined in TightSealGeom.cpp.

std::optional< Eigen::VectorXd > wmtk::components::simwild::tight_seal::equal_distances_point ( const Eigen::MatrixXd &  D)

The point of a simplex where k distance functions, each interpolated linearly from its values at the simplex's k corners, are all equal: for a triangle (k = 3) the triple point of three regions, for a tet (k = 4) the quadruple point of four (tight seal v2 note, section 4a-i).

D(r, c) is function r at corner c. Returns the point's barycentric coordinates (they sum to 1), or nothing if the system is singular. The point lies inside the simplex if all are > 0.

Where a 1-Lipschitz f reaches level along the segment [a, b], coming from a, which is below the level if from_below (f(a) < level), else above it (f(a) > level).

Sphere traced from a, each step |f - level| + step_floor (at least step_floor, so the trace ends; no step goes more than step_floor past the level), until f is at the level or past it; the last step is then bisected to the level. Nothing if f(a) is not on the side from_below gives, or if f stays on that side up to b.

The region "split_fill" gives a cell, from its corners' own labels.

The corners' region if they agree. Otherwise an edge between two of them was not split, because the distances to their regions are equal at one of its ends; the cell then takes the one region that every corner's tie set holds. If they share several (an exact tie between regions) or none, the choice is arbitrary: the first region they share, or else the first corner's.

ties(v): the regions within the tie tolerance of the nearest one at v, ascending. Returns the region and how it was chosen: 0 the corners agree, 1 their tie sets, 2 arbitrarily.
