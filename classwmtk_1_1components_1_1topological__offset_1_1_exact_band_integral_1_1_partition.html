Detailed Description

One cell's partition, kept by the caller between value() and value_grad_hess() of that cell: each partitions t (partition(), the candidates and the convex pieces) before integrating, and a partition is a function of t's 12 coordinates, in their order, and of the input alone. Given a Partition, a call whose t equals, bit for bit, the t the Partition was made for (by this integral) reads it; any other call partitions t into it. The value is therefore the one the call would compute without it.

Why: the smoother asks for J at x (value(), the line search) and then for its derivatives at the same x (value_grad_hess()), each partitioning every band cell of the ring. MEASURED (Geneva base, inward, the smoothing pass after the split, sampled): partition() was 51% of the busy time, 20% inside value_grad_hess() and 31% inside value().
