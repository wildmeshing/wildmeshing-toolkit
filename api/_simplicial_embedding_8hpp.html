Detailed Description

Simplicial embedding of a subcomplex K of a triangle or tet mesh. K is simplicially embedded if every simplex of the mesh whose vertices all lie in K belongs to K (K is the full subcomplex on its vertices). Then every simplex outside K has a vertex off K: no cell outside K has all its vertices in K, and no edge outside K joins two vertices of K.

K is given by membership tests, asked about the mesh as it is at the time. simplicial_embedding splits, in this order, every cell outside K whose facets all lie in K, then (3D) every face outside K whose edges all do, then every edge outside K whose two vertices do; each pass lists its simplices first, then splits them. The mesh's split hooks place the new vertex and set the new simplices' attributes, and the tests must count none of the new simplices in K (the new vertex is inside a simplex outside K); the three passes then leave K embedded.

topological_offset embeds its input complex (its labels), tight_seal_geom the union of its regions (by tag).
