Detailed Description

The collapse energy rule's before-half, per thread (collapse_before_vertex()): the sum of tri_energy() over v1's ring, the faces the collapse reshapes or removes, and the faces of v2's ring outside it, sorted, which keep their slots, corners and labels and so their energy. The 3D twin is CollapseVolume, less its "surface" and use_rest_pose fields (not ported, see tri_energy()).
