Detailed Description

template<int N>
class wmtk::history::BasicReplay< N >

A complex driven by the edits of a History: a surface (N = 3) or a curve network (N = 2).

Starts from the history's snapshot and applies edits one at a time, with the semantics the meshes give their operations (see EditKind), so after the k-th edit it holds exactly the recording mesh's state after its k-th committed operation: the same vertex and face ids, the same corner order in every face, the same positions.
