Detailed Description

A vertex's part of the energy's band term (TopoOffsetTetMesh::band_cell_term()), the vertex moving: 

E(x) = sum over the vertex's band cells t of  Vol_t(x) * m_t(x),
m_t(x) = min over ALL input triangles P of (1/4) sum over t's 4 corners q of
         (d_P(q) - delta) / delta,

the four corners being x and the cell's three fixed ones, Vol_t the cell's signed volume, positive on a valid cell. Each cell's term is band_cell_term() at x, so the solve descends exactly the cell terms E sums.

Vol_t(x) = (q1 - x) . ((q2 - q1) x (q3 - q1)) / 6 is affine in x: its gradient is -(q2 - q1) x (q3 - q1) / 6 and its Hessian zero. The gradient and Hessian of m_t are the minimising triangle P's: grad m_t = grad d_P(x) / (4 delta), hess m_t = hess d_P(x) / (4 delta). So grad E_t = m_t grad Vol + Vol grad m_t and hess E_t = grad Vol grad m_t^T + grad m_t grad Vol^T + Vol hess m_t. The outer-product pair is indefinite in general; polysolve's Newton regularises, and the AMIPS term beside it carries the shape.

Every value, gradient and Hessian equals, bit for bit, that of a min_mean_distance() call per cell and evaluation; the work behind them is cut twice (exact_minima()):

  • the last point's minima are kept, so value, gradient and Hessian at one x (polysolve asks for each separately, and again in its line search) search once;
  • each cell searches only its certified candidates (ball_candidates()), valid at every x in a ball around the point they were built at. r, the ball's radius, is the distance from that point to the nearest plane through a cell's three fixed corners: the largest ball around it in which none of these cells can invert. The solver evaluates only points where no ring cell is inverted (polysolve tests is_step_valid() before each trial value), so it leaves this ball only away from the nearest face. Correctness does not depend on r, only the speed. Outside the ball the candidates are rebuilt around the new point; where no ball can be certified there (r <= 0 or not finite), that evaluation runs min_mean_distance() itself. Measured on the cube (cube_on, 3 turns, serial): 7.45M evaluations, 57% at the last point; of the rest 96% inside the ball, with 1.37 candidates per cell; the whole tree 8 times. Smoothing 68 s -> 15 s, the run 76 s -> 23 s, the output bit-identical.

EXPERIMENTAL_band_volume_rule "front_flux": the front form of the same band term (ExactBandIntegral.hpp): the value is (sum over the band's boundary faces f through x of int_f G.n dA.

  • sum over the cells t of D_t, for the flagged cells) / delta, which differs from the "exact" rule's sum over the cells of J_t / delta by the flux through the faces that do not hold x, a constant in x: a face holding x lies in two cells of x's ring, so its fluxes cancel unless one side is not a band cell. Gradient and Hessian are those of E(B) / delta in x (ExactBandIntegral::front_face(), summed over faces); they have no D_t term. D_t is evaluated for a cell flagged by ExactBandIntegral::front_flux_flag(), or whose bound on d exceeds front_flux_reach() (the flag's polytopes hold the bisector points with d <= reach only): U_t = ExactBandIntegral::distance_bound(), kept per cell with the x it was computed at and read as U_t + |x - x_U| at a later x, a bound too (each d_P is 1-Lipschitz and only x moves), the search repeated where that exceeds the reach. Why U_t and not d(x) + max |q - x| (d 1-Lipschitz): MEASURED (stored cube_out band, 52957 cells, reach 2 delta), the latter put 10618 band cells beyond the reach, 5.0 D_t evaluations per front vertex, where the cube outward has no region overlaps on its offset side at all. The certified ball and the exact rule's integrals are not used. Any exception from the band term is kept (front_flux_error()) before it propagates: the shared smoother takes an exception out of a solve for a failed line search.
