Detailed Description

The map between the first and the last state of a SurfaceHistory.

Built from the history alone, one local map per operation (BijectiveRemesh). A split maps its new faces affinely onto the face they subdivide, with the new vertex where the history put it; a collapse and a swap map through a joint embedding of the faces they touch, before and after, on a shared outline, Tutte's lowered in distortion by SLIM within a scaffold (see Scaffold); a vertex move follows the options' smoothing (see Smoothing). A consolidation renumbers faces.
