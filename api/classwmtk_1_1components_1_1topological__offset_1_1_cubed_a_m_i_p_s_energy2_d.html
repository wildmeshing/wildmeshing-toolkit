Detailed Description

The 2D twin of CubedAMIPSEnergy3D: w * sum over faces of area AMIPS^3 (TopoOffsetTriMesh::tri_energy(), whose caller passes w = offset_amips_weight / stop_energy^3), AMIPS the 2D engine's first-power AMIPS2D.

Each face's area AMIPS^3 is (sqrt3/4) T AMIPS^2 with T = (2/3) sum |e|^2 over its three edges (TopoOffsetTriMesh::area_amips3()) – the area never from a determinant, as in 3D. Faces in the shared smoother's convention: 6 doubles with the moving vertex first, its two entries replaced by x. Derivatives by the chain rule from the engine's AMIPS2D ones. A step that inverts a face is invalid, as for the engine's AMIPSEnergy2D.
