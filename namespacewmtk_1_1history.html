enum class wmtk::history::EditKind : uint8_t
strong

The kinds of edit a complex goes through.

A complex is a surface, made of triangles, or a curve network, made of segments (see History); either way its cells are called faces here. Every kind is described on the complex alone – vertex and face ids, positions – never on the mesh class that performed it, so a tet mesh can report the changes to its tracked surface, and a triangle mesh the changes to its tracked curves, in the same terms as a TriMesh reports its own. The ids are the recording mesh's slots: a split names the slots it was handed, and a consolidation renumbers every live vertex and face by rank, as the meshes do.

Enumerator
split_edge 

v = {v0, v1, new_v}. faces = {f, new_f} for each face on the edge: f keeps the v0 half (v1 renamed to new_v in place) and new_f is f with v0 renamed to new_v. On a curve the face on the edge is the segment (v0, v1) itself, if it is one.

split_face 

Surfaces only. v = {v0, v1, v2, new_v}. faces = {f, new_f1, new_f2}: f with v0, new_f1 with v1 and new_f2 with v2 renamed to new_v, each in place.

collapse_edge 

v = {removed, kept}. Faces with both endpoints go; removed is renamed to kept elsewhere. Duplicate faces this leaves at kept merge into the smallest fid, and a vertex it leaves without faces is retired – unless, on a curve, it is a feature point.

swap_edge 

Surfaces only. v = {v0, v1, a, b}, faces = {fa, fb}: edge (v0,v1) becomes (a,b). In fa, which holds the apex b, v1 is renamed to a; in fb, which holds a, v0 is renamed to b.

move_vertices 

Only vertex positions change (smoothing, or a sweep outside any operation).

consolidate 

Live vertices and faces renumbered by rank, in id order.

replace_faces 

v = the corners of the faces it adds, a face's worth each; faces = the faces it removes, then the ones it adds, as many as v has faces. A change no other kind describes – a tracked surface re-derived, say – with no correspondence between what goes and what comes. A vertex it leaves without faces is retired, as by a collapse; one an added face names is alive, its position among the moves if it was not alive before.

relabel 

v = the new id of every vertex slot, kNoVertex for one that goes; faces = the new id of every face slot, kNoVertex for one that goes, or empty; moves = the position of every new vertex. Vertices are renamed in place in every face – several may become one – and a face left with a repeated corner goes; the faces that stay take the ids faces gives, or are renumbered by rank. Several faces given one id – duplicates a weld leaves – become one, with the first's corners. A weld of coincident vertices, a change of coordinates, a reordering: each face keeps its corners, so a point keeps its barycentric coordinates.

embed 

The complex is replaced by one lying on it – the same surface or curves, cut differently (an insertion into a tet mesh, an arrangement of segments). v = the corners of its faces, a face's worth each, in its own vertex ids from 0; faces = per face, how many faces before it lies on, then those, and after them its features, how many then their vertices; moves = the position of every vertex. A point stays where it is in space.

A curve application's history from its input files as read, to the network it inserts.

The files' segments, as io::read_edge_mesh read them; a relabel to the welded network the application ran on; the input simplification's collapses on that network; and a relabel onto the rows the simplification left. A user's point on the input is then a segment of the files themselves. The feature points are the welded network's vertices with other than two segments – curve ends, junctions, input points – each named by the first vertex of the files that became it.

Parameters
readthe files as read – the curve files' vertices and segments, then the point files' vertices – and where each went in V_in, E_in
V_in,E_inthe welded network (two or three columns; z is dropped)
collapsesutils::simplify_segments' collapses on V_in, E_in; empty if it did not run
V_out,E_outthe network the simplification left, the one inserted
surviving_verticesfor each row of V_out, its row of V_in
Exceptions
ifthe collapses do not leave E_out's segments

A surface application's history from its input file as read.

The file's triangles, as io::read_triangle_mesh read them; a relabel to the welded mesh the application ran on; then the history the application's mesh recorded from it (TriMesh:: start_history right after create_mesh).
