Detailed Description

Helpers of the geometric tight seal, SimWildMeshTri::tight_seal_geom (AnnotationsTri.cpp) and SimWildMesh::tight_seal_geom (AnnotationsTet.cpp), which keep the operation itself. The helpers below need no mesh; those that do are private members of the two mesh classes, defined in TightSealGeom.cpp.

Certifies that a 1-Lipschitz function f stays on one side of level over a whole cell (a triangle in 2D, a tet in 3D), conservatively: true only if proven.

at_most: max over the cell of f <= level; otherwise: min over the cell of f > level. The conservative circle (sphere) subdivision of topological_offset's Circle.hpp and Sphere.hpp: a circle of radius rho around c holds only values within rho of f(c). Starting from the circle around the cell's bounding square (cube), a circle whose values are all on the right side (by the relative margin certify_margin) is certified; the cell is refuted if a corner is not (corner_values, with the margin), or if a circle's centre lies in the cell with f on the wrong side; any other circle is refined into the circles around its square's 2^dim sub-squares that overlap the cell, and refutes the cell once max_depth levels down.

Sphere tracing of a 1-Lipschitz function f along a + t dir, t in [0, L), towards the level set f = level.

From a point at f, the level set is at least |f - level| away in every direction, so a step that long along the segment cannot cross it. from_below: f(a) < level and each step is level - f; otherwise f(a) > level and each step is f - level. Returns the first t (of a + t dir) where |f - level| <= tol, or nothing if the trace leaves [0, L) first: past the far end, the level set is not on the segment; behind a, f(a) is already beyond the level.
