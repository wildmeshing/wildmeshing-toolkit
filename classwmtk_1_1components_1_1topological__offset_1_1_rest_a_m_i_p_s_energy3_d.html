Detailed Description

The 3D twin of RestAMIPSEnergy2D: AMIPS of a tet against its rest shape. 

E(x) = w * sum over cells of tr(F^T F) / det(F)^(2/3),   F = A(x) * Rinv

A(x) = [q1 - x, q2 - x, q3 - x] with the moving vertex first (the shared smoother's convention), Rinv the inverse rest Jacobian in the same corner order. det^(2/3) is what makes the 3D form scale-invariant, as det^1 does in 2D; the minimum is 3 at F = I, the same scale as the shared AMIPSEnergy3D against the regular tet, so the two terms sum 1:1. det F <= 0 is invalid: value NaN and is_step_valid false. F is affine in x, so gradient and Hessian are the exact chain through the closed-form derivatives of e / d^(2/3) in F.
