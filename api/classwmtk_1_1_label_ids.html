Detailed Description

The labels (inputs) a simplex lies on: sorted, distinct ids. What the surface records carry (SurfaceTagAttributes::labels()), what the envelopes are asked about (InputEnvelopes::containment()), and what the feature edges and points carry. The invariant: a tracked simplex stays within eps of EVERY label it has.
