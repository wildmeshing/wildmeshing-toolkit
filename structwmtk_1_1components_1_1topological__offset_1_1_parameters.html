Detailed Description

What the offset needs on top of the parameters every wmtk optimizer shares.

The optimization phase reuses wmtk::TriOptimizerMesh / wmtk::TetOptimizerMesh, so everything that phase reads – target edge length, the split/collapse thresholds derived from it, smoothing weights and pass count, the sizing knobs, the debug switch – comes from wmtk::OptimizerParameters and is not restated here. The bounding box stays here because its type and meaning differ per application.

Every key below means the same thing in 2D and 3D; the two pipelines are the same algorithm one dimension apart, and a key that only one of them read would be a difference in the algorithm.

Member Function Documentation

◆ front_conv_frac()

double wmtk::components::topological_offset::Parameters::front_conv_frac ( ) const
inline

The convergence epsilon as a FRACTION of target_distance, which is the form the dimensionless relative error OffsetPotential::relative_residual() is compared against. A mean of squared relative errors is below front_conv_frac()^2 exactly when the same mean taken in lengths is below front_conv^2 – the two differ by target_distance^2 on both sides – so which form the code uses is a matter of where the division sits, not of what is being asked.

◆ band_volume_rule

std::string wmtk::components::topological_offset::Parameters::band_volume_rule = "corner_bound"

EXPERIMENTAL (2026-10-12), 3D. The band term b(t) of a band cell in the energy (TopoOffsetTetMesh::band_cell_term()). "corner_bound" (the default): vol times the min over all input triangles P of the corner mean of (d_P - delta) / delta, an upper bound on the cell's part of int_B (d - delta) / delta dV that no split raises. "exact": J_t / delta, J_t = int_t (d - delta) dV in closed form (ExactBandIntegral); an upper bound only on its rule-(a) pieces. "front_flux": the operations as "exact", the front smoother's band term in the front form (flux of a field G with div G = d - delta through the band's boundary faces at the vertex, plus the bisector jumps D_t of its flagged cells). "exact_swept" (2026-10-22): "exact"'s energy, but each operation's rule reads the band integral's exact change where the band changes – value() on the tets a moving vertex's boundary faces of the band sweep, or on the cone over a flip's changed faces – instead of integrating whole cells before and after; the smoother searches on that same exact change (its derivatives by Reynolds' transport theorem over the band's boundary faces, or the ring cells' where a curved bisector crosses one), and its move is accepted on it, halved up to 4 times. See the spec doc and TopoOffsetTetMesh, EXACT SWEPT CHANGE. "surface" (2026-10-27): no band term; the offset term is a surface integral over the front, L / front_conv^2 (L the bounding box diagonal) sum over the offset faces of (A_f / |S|) sum over the stencil of (d - target_distance)^2 (TopoOffsetTetMesh::surface_face_term()). See the spec doc.

◆ coarsen_guard

◆ front_measure

Which measure the loop exits on and refines by, in 3D and in 2D; see the spec. "vertex_ring" (the default): at each front vertex the RING MEASURE, the root mean square of the face measures (face_offset_term()) of its incident offset faces – in 2D of the chord measures (edge_offset_term()) of its front chords – every simplex weighted equally. ◆ growth_mode

3D (2026-10-21; "linear" was EXPERIMENTAL_incremental_growth): how the loop grows the offset when the march traces the front below target_distance (construction_mode "max_marchable_fallback", the target not below the maximum marchable distance), from the traced distance d_0. ◆ settle_mark

std::string wmtk::components::topological_offset::Parameters::settle_mark = "vertex_edge"

EXPERIMENTAL (2026-10-09), read only under settle_refinement: the test that marks the front on a settled turn. "vertex": a front vertex's own |d - target_distance| exceeds front_conv; its sizing is halved. "vertex_edge" (the default): "vertex", and also both ends of a front edge whose ends are within front_conv and whose midpoint is not have their sizing halved (they do not hold the exit). "vertex_split": "vertex", and also a front face whose corners are within front_conv but an edge midpoint or its centroid is not has its worst edge split before the next turn's first smoothing pass (needs EXPERIMENTAL_front_pocket_split). "max": a face's max of |d - target_distance| exceeds front_conv (exact up to rounding; an upper bound on rule-(a) pieces, see ExactBandIntegral::max_distance()). "rms": a face's share of the offset excess exceeds its bar energy, X_f = (1/2 delta) int_f (d - delta)^2 cos(theta) dA > front_conv^2 area(f) / (2 delta), i.e. the mean over f of (d - delta)^2 cos(theta) exceeds front_conv^2 (theta between the face's normal, out of the band, and grad d; signed), by the 3-edge-midpoint rule. "stencil" (2026-10-28): a face with a point of its offset stencil (stencil_order, EXPERIMENTAL_stencil_type) at or beyond front_conv, (d - target_distance)^2 >= front_conv^2, or unmeasurable, has its corners' sizing halved; the exit needs none, i.e. every stencil point within front_conv. See the spec doc.

◆ settle_refinement

bool wmtk::components::topological_offset::Parameters::settle_refinement = true

EXPERIMENTAL (2026-10-09), 3D. Refinement and exit wait for the front to settle: a turn is settled when the offset energy (E without its AMIPS term) fell by less than the bar energy b = front_conv^2 A / (2 target_distance), A the front's area – the offset term's excess for a front lying front_conv off the level set. On a settled turn every front face whose distance from the level set (the face's min of d, and its max of d, ExactBandIntegral::max_distance()) exceeds front_conv has its corners halved; exit = settled and no face over. The energy criterion's own refinement and exit are off. See the spec doc.

◆ settle_stall_eps

double wmtk::components::topological_offset::Parameters::settle_stall_eps = 0.1

EXPERIMENTAL_band_volume_rule "surface" only: the settle test's stall threshold. O* = the max over the front's stencil points of (d - target_distance)^2; a turn is settled when O* < front_conv^2 or O*_prev - O* <= settle_stall_eps (O* - front_conv^2) – TetWild's stall test on the max offset error. See the spec doc.
