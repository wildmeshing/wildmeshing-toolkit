Detailed Description

EXPERIMENTAL_band_volume_rule "surface": a vertex's part of the offset term, the vertex x moving, over its live offset faces (x, q1, q2): 

S(x) = W sum_f A_f(x) m_f(x),   m_f = (1 / |S|) sum over the stencil points q_k of r(q_k)^2,

r = (d - delta) / delta the potential's relative_residual(), q_k = w_k0 x + w_k1 q1 + w_k2 q2 (interior_face_stencil() or fem_face_stencil()), A_f = |N| / 2 with N = (q1 - x) x (q2 - x) affine in x, and W the term's weight (L / front_conv_frac()^2, L the bounding box diagonal, TopoOffsetTetMesh::surface_term_weight()). So W A_f m_f = L (A_f / |S|) sum (d(q_k) - delta)^2 / front_conv^2, the spec's weighted O(f). Under the FEM stencil the mean is weighted by the points' lumped area weights mu_k (fem_face_stencil_weights()): m_f = sum_k mu_k r(q_k)^2, and every 1 / |S| below reads mu_k. Exact derivatives: grad m = (2 / |S|) sum r w_k0 grad r(q_k), hess m = (2 / |S|) sum w_k0^2 (grad r grad r^T + r hess r); grad (A m) = A grad m + m grad A, hess (A m) = A hess m + grad A grad m^T + grad m grad A^T + m hess A. A face of zero area contributes 0 and no area derivative; a sample whose residual is not finite makes the value +inf. The value is TopoOffsetTetMesh::surface_face_term()'s arithmetic with x as the face's first corner.
