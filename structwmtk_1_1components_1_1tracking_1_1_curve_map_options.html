Smoothing wmtk::components::tracking::CurveMapOptions::smoothing = Smoothing::flatten

How the map follows a vertex smoothing step (EditKind::move_vertices): barycentric keeps each point at its segment and barycentric coordinates, riding along with the vertex; flatten keeps it where it was along the curve, by arc length between the vertices that stay, as every other operation is mapped.
