Detailed Description

The offset curve's edges turned from the field: over offset_surface_edges(), the edges whose front_edge_field_angle() exceeds foldover_angle, the unmeasurable ones (NaN, not counted as turned), and the worst angle with its edge's midpoint. The end-of-run [turned edges] warning and growth_mode "smart"'s test read it. 3D's turned_face_census().
