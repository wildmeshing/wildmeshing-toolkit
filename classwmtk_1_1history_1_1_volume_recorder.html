Detailed Description

Records a VolumeHistory alongside a TrackedSurfaceRecorder. An operation captures the tets around it before it changes the mesh and at its commit point (TetMesh does both), and commits them here with the range of surface edits its surface commit made; one that made none is not recorded. Commits may come concurrently: they are kept, encoded, in the order they come, and ordered by surface edit when read. The mesh's vertices get ids of their own here, which a consolidation (renumber) does not change, so a vertex's position is written again only when it moved.
