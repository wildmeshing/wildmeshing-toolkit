Detailed Description

A point on an application's input.

A history that starts at input files (every application but simwild on a tet mesh) names it by a triangle of those files – the triangles of all of them, in order, as the files hold them – and barycentric coordinates with respect to that triangle's corners in file order. One that starts at a tet mesh's tracked surface (simwild on a .msh), which is no element of a file, names it by three vertices of that mesh – the corners of a tracked face – and barycentric coordinates in their order, as an OutputPoint of a tet output does.
