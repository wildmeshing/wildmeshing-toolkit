Detailed Description

The map between the first and the last state of a CurveHistory.

Built from the history alone, one local map per operation: BijectiveRemesh in one dimension, where a joint flattening is exact. An operation changes the segments around a few vertices; between the vertices it leaves in place – untouched ones, and junctions, ends and feature points, which split the curves into chains – the curve before and the curve after are two polylines between the same two vertices, and the local map takes one onto the other by normalised arc length: a bijection by construction, with constant stretch along the chain. A split, a collapse and a smoothing step are all mapped so. An operation that changes the topology leaves chains with no counterpart; their segments are mapped by relabelling where they still exist, and counted (CurveMapWarnings::topology). A chain a collapse contracted – two anchors collapsed into one, or a loop of three closed into a doubled segment – holds the collapsed segment, which no longer exists: it maps by arc length onto what the chain became, the segments it merged into or the survivor alone. A relabel and a consolidation renumber segments; an embed maps each point to the same place on the segment it lies on.

Sets a chain's starts and total from its segments' lengths, each counting for at least a billionth of the chain's mean segment – or all alike, if the chain has no length. A segment of no length in doubles, two vertices the arrangement set apart by less than their rounding, then keeps a sliver of the chain, and the map stays one to one; any other chain is measured by its arc length exactly.
