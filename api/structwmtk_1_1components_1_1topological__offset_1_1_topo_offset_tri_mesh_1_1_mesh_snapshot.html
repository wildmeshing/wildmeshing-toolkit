Detailed Description

An in-memory image of the mesh, to put it back as it was (for growth_mode "smart": the revert after a failed attempt). The 2D twin of TopoOffsetTetMesh::MeshSnapshot. Taken on a consolidated mesh: the faces in fid order, each as oriented_tri_vids() gives it – the order the edge slots (3 fid + j) are keyed on – and copies of every per-element collection over the live slots: the vertex group (m_vertex_attribute, m_vertex_extra), the edge group (m_edge_attribute, m_edge_extra) and the face group (m_face_attribute, m_face_extra). 2D has no cell collection apart from the face group, which holds what 3D's m_tet_attribute does.
