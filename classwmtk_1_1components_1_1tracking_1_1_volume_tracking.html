Detailed Description

The map of a tet mesh's volume between the mesh its volume history starts from and the one it ends at, in the terms of their files: Tracking's counterpart for the volume (simwild, tetwild with volume_history_output).

Built from the surface and volume histories the application saved (see VolumeMap for how), and the two meshes, to name points by: the one the history starts from, which the application writes as <output>_history_input.msh, and the one it ends at – simwild's <output>.msh; for tetwild, the mesh before its filter, <output>_history_output.msh, of which <output>_final.msh holds the part kept (a point of the part dropped has no tet there). On the memory route the histories are the bytes of report["history"] and report["volume_history"], and the meshes the output meshes history_input, and mesh (simwild) or history_output (tetwild). A point keeps its region: where it lands on a face between two, it is named by a tet of its own. One with no tet to land in – outside the mesh given – comes back with no tet (kNoId), as does one that is no point: of a tet the mesh does not have, or with coordinates that are not numbers. Without the meshes, it maps positions (map_positions()).

Its const methods may be called from several threads at once (VolumeMap's are; the meshes are not changed). A copy shares the map, its built operations and its stats, and the meshes.
